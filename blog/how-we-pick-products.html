How We Pick Products

No magic algorithm. No paid placements. Just a strict filter applied to real Amazon.in data.

The Filter

  1. Category selection — We cover what Indians actually buy: kitchen appliances, electronics, fitness gear, smartphones.
  2. Minimum review threshold — 1,000+ verified ratings (exception: niche categories where 500+ with 4.5★+).
  3. Minimum rating — 4.0★ average. Products at 3.8-3.9★ only included with clear caveats.
  4. Real discount verification — We calculate: (MRP - Current Price) / MRP. Fake MRPs flagged and excluded.
  5. Availability check — Must be in stock with Prime/free delivery option.
  6. Review sentiment scan — We read recent 1-2★ reviews for recurring defects. If a pattern exists, we note it.

What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ Accept free review units from brands
  • ❌ Prioritize higher-commission products
  • ❌ Use "Amazon's Choice" as a quality signal (it's often just sales velocity)
  • ❌ Hide negative feedback — every product has a Cons section
  • ❌ Create fake urgency ("Only 2 left!") unless stock is genuinely low

Price Accuracy

Prices are checked daily from Amazon.in product pages. MRP is taken from the listing. Discount % = (MRP - Deal Price) / MRP × 100. If MRP looks inflated (common in fitness), we note it.

Updates

Deal pages updated when: price changes >5%, new review milestone hit (10K, 50K, 100K), or recurring defect reported. Last update date shown on every product page.

Trust But Verify

We encourage you to click through to Amazon, read recent reviews yourself, and compare prices. Our job is to narrow 100,000 options to 18 worth your time — your job is the final 5 minutes of due diligence.
