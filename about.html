Why This Site Exists

Indian e-commerce is flooded with fake reviews, inflated MRPs, and "deals" that aren't deals. I built DealHub India because I was tired of seeing friends and family waste money on products that looked good on paper but disappointed in reality.

Every product on this site is verified against real Amazon.in data — actual ratings, real review counts, live prices checked daily. No affiliate pressure, no sponsored placements, no fake urgency. If a product isn't worth buying, I'll tell you why.

How We Pick Products

  • Real Amazon data only — ratings, reviews, prices pulled from live listings
  • Minimum 1,000+ reviews — or exceptional value in niche categories
  • Minimum 4.0★ rating — with exceptions clearly noted
  • Verified discount — we calculate actual savings vs. MRP
  • Category relevance — what Indian buyers actually search for
